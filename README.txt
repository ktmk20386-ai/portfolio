AKASH S — PERSONAL PORTFOLIO

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
3. Keep the css, js, and images folders alongside index.html.

FILES
- index.html        Main portfolio page
- css/style.css     Responsive styling and light/dark themes
- js/script.js      Mobile menu, theme switch, project filters, details, copy-email
- images/akash-profile.jpg   Your supplied profile photo

CUSTOMISE
- Update the About text and project cards in index.html.
- The three project cards are sample concepts for presentation; replace them with your real projects and working links before submitting.
- Skill bars are illustrative only.
- Google Fonts is optional; the site falls back to system fonts if unavailable.
- The contact form is intentionally omitted; email and telephone links are provided.

Made with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks.
